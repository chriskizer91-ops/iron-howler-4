These views use orthographic cameras on the sheet's exact pixel grid (1 px = 2 cm). Raise Sheet overlay to lay the original drawing over both models and check the match.

What to expect

Front and side match closely. The rear drawing disagrees with the front (wider stance, two ears), so its colours are warped onto the model at bake time. Tops and inner faces aren't visible in any view, so they take the nearest sheet tone.

Files

wraith-fresh/models/ has the full-resolution GLBs and blender/ the .blend files. The rebuild pipeline is scripts/run_all.sh.
