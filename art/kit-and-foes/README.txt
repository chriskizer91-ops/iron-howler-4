KNOWN DIFFERENCES / LIMITS
These are AI-generated matching exterior reference views, not renders of a single shared CAD/3D model. Exact internal geometry, hidden surfaces, decal/chip placement and all point-to-point correspondences are not guaranteed across views, even though canvas sizes and outer envelopes are aligned. Small residual perspective and baked material shading can remain. Check/reconcile interior landmarks before tracing a production mesh or projecting paint.
The top-view rule has a geometric conflict: at the same physical scale, top-view vertical extent represents length, while front/back vertical extent represents height. C04 and F04 retain the same metres-to-pixels scale; their highest/lowest rows therefore differ from the elevations.
Weapon surfaces are fictional game-prop exterior designs; some fine functional-looking details were simplified while retaining equipment counts and silhouettes.
Character-sheet size illustrations and incidental mass/performance text are concept-art annotations. The modeling sizes listed in this README are authoritative.
