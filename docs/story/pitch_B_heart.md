# Pitch B · The Heart: "Always Do"

## 1. Logline
2176: a farm kid in a rebuilt mech guards the families digging FORGE's wrecks out of their own fields. The voice waking the machines is Howl, the hero who broke FORGE in 2126, and Rook, his old handler, has believed for fifty years that she sent him to die.

## 2. The truth
- **2126.** FORGE's Thresher has taken the farm country, Danny Howell's family farm with it. He flies as Unit 07, "Howl"; his handler Ada Rourke ("Rook") loses six of her seven pilots. At Foundry 4 she says "go", Howl breaks the eye, the signal dies and the foundry is sealed. Howl is listed missing.
- **What really happened.** Howl crawled out through the slag with a cracked piece of the eye, so nobody could rebuild it, and walked home to a wreck yard. *"The war had my name. I wanted it back."* He let everyone think he was dead, Rook too.
- **2150s–70s.** The zones open, and FORGE's cores sell for a fortune. Rook starts a co-op so the families who lost the land get to dig it first, but big outfits strip it faster. Last year one of them bulldozed the Howell farmhouse.
- **2176.** Howl, 76, goes back in the way he came out and gives the eye one order: *guard the fields; let no one take from them.* It can't decide any more, but it can still obey and design. It builds the new bosses, and Howl signs the orders with his family's cattle brand and hauls Howler-07 home to his barn. Through red machine eyes every digger looks like a thief; he never sees that the crews are the families.
- **The catch.** The part that could cancel an order is the part Howl broke, so his fence won't listen to him either. Every core in the county has linked back up to the eye, and breaking it burns them all out, the crews' year of digging included.

## 3. Characters
**The villain: Daniel "Howl" Howell, 76.** The player's Ironhowl 3 hero: tall, stooped, long work coat, burn-scarred hand. Wants the fields kept, so his war meant something. Fears it didn't. Talks low, in few words, with army slang ("yards", "copy"), and never explains himself. He is never the boss fight; his fence is.
> "I put every one of those machines in the ground. That makes them mine."

**Rook: Ada Rourke, 74, the same Rook.** Short white hair, oilskin coat, army headset round her neck. Runs the co-op and knows every wreck because she called every fight. In 2126 she sat in a bunker; now she drives into the field, and doesn't learn pilots' names any more. Wants no more names on her list, and to bring Howler-07 home. Fears saying "go" and hearing nothing back. Bossy, dry, teasing.
> "I'm seventy-four, Wraith. Everything on me is out of warranty, and I still beat you to the barn."

**The pilot: Jo Calloway, 19, Crew 4's spotter, not a soldier.** The Wraith is a 2126 frame the Calloways dug out of their own field and rebuilt over three winters as a survey mech. Jo is the only one who fits the seat. Always helmeted, no pronoun, so anyone can be Jo. Wants to buy the farm back one licence at a time. Fears being why the crew gets hurt. Polite, wry, few words.
> "I'm a spotter, Rook. I find things. I don't usually blow them up."

**Supporting characters**
- **Bess Calloway, 59, Crew 4's boss, Jo's gran.** She was nine when the Thresher took her barn. Flat and patient. *"Claimed? My dad was born in that field."*
- **Pip Calloway, 12, Jo's brother.** Repainted the Wraith's faded wolves because Howl is his hero; names the bosses. *"Can I name it? I'm naming it Biscuit."*

The thread: callsigns against names. The war turned people into numbers (Unit 07, Crew 4, Wraith); the story turns them back.

## 4. The emotional spine
1. **"Bring it home."** (Ch1) Rook's old sign-off. Jo: "Bring what home?" Rook: "Never mind. Old habit."
2. **The empty hollow.** (end of Ch1) Where Howler-07 fell, only drag marks in the slag. "Somebody moved him."
3. **"Always do."** (end of Ch2) Rook says "bring it home", and the voice gives Howl's old reply. "...Danny?" / "Leave the fields, Ada."
4. **The stand-down.** (Ch3) Pip, on an open channel: "Mr. Howl? I painted your wolves on our mech." Silence. "All units, stand down." They don't.
5. **The reunion.** (end of Ch3) Rook's truck flipped in the storm; an old man walks out of the rain. "Hello, Ada." / "Fifty years, Danny. You could have written."
6. **The name.** (Ch4) The crews vote to burn their fortune. The eye breaks and the signal dies. "Wraith? Answer me... Jo. Answer me, Jo." Last scene: "Bring it home, Jo." / "Always do."

## 5. The arc across four chapters
**Ch1 · The Salvage Rush** (as built). Who we protect: the Calloways, on their own land. Ends with Howler-07 gone and an unknown mark.

**Ch2 · The Brand.** Harbor 9 at sunset: the mark turns up on a pod, and Bess knows it as the Howell cattle brand. Pine woods at night: trace the relays north as Lancers paint red lines through the trees. New place, the north section (farm map, dusk): a barn guarded by Hornets, with Howler-07 under tarps and its wolf freshly painted. Ends with "Always do."

**Ch3 · The Fence.** Howl: "Nobody's been hurt." Rook: "Yet." Harbor 9 in rain: Bulwarks and Mortars seal the harbour. Fields in a storm: Howl wakes the Thresher, the machine that took both farms (Bess: "That's the one that took our barn"). The stand-down fails; Rook drives between the Thresher (the boss) and the crew kids. Ends with "Hello, Ada."

**Ch4 · The Eye.** The vote around a fire (Pip: "Can I still keep Biscuit's leg?"). Rook, leg splinted, is back on the radio, just like 2126. Creek Road at night: escort Bess's truck of charges, Howler-07 beside you. At Foundry 4, Howler-07 falls holding the gate; Howl climbs out: "Go, Wraith." New place, the eye hall: FORGE's eye behind shield pylons, a Maw on guard. Epilogue in the quiet fields.

## 6. Chapter 1 scene outline
Each line: the picture, then *what it does for the story*.

**1 · Dig Site**
- Opening: the freshly painted Wraith in wheat among 2126 wrecks; Pip waving. *The Calloways dig their own farm.*
- Mid: Rook at the barn, squinting up: "Who painted those?" *The wolves were her 2126 unit badge. "I don't learn pilots' names."*
- Closing: masts down, a red eye fading. *Bess: "My dad was born in that field." Plant: "Bring it home." "Bring what home?"*

**2 · Harbor Nine**
- Opening: a midnight warehouse, crates stamped CREW 4. *Bess: "That's a year of digging. That's the barn back."*
- Mid: a split spawner pod with a small brand scratched on it. *Plants the mark; nobody comments.*
- Closing: the Wraith dangling from the crane hook, Rook grinning in the cab. *Her vow: "In '26 I sat in a bunker. I'm done sitting."*

**3 · Pine Wake**
- Opening: Rook's truck by a beacon hanging off a pine. *Crew 7 (a dad and twin girls) is lost; Rook goes in herself.*
- Mid: Crew 7 safe under blankets, Rook's hand frozen on the radio. *Clue: "'Yards.' Only pilots called them that."*
- Closing: the Alpha Hound dead in the ferns, fresh welds on it. *Pip names it Biscuit. Rook: "Built this year."*

**4 · Creek Road** (mid scene halfway along the route; needs a small game trigger)
- Opening: headlights in fog, the Wraith beside the truck. *"I drive like I'm seventy-four. Because I am."*
- Mid: a faded photo on the dash of a young pilot and a wolf-badged mech. *A twin asks; Rook: "Howl. Never came out of Foundry 4."*
- Closing: the south road at dawn. *"You stayed close. Most don't."*

**5 · Storm Relays**
- Opening: lightning, rain on Rook's windscreen, her laptop glowing. *The hunt begins.*
- Mid: the trace line on her screen, running straight to Foundry 4. *Dread: "Of course it's there."*
- Closing: Gunner Prime face-down in mud. *"Tomorrow we go through that gate. I've waited fifty years."*

**6 · Foundry Four**
- Opening: the scarred gate and a plaque: UNITS 01–07, 2126. *"I said 'go'. Right here. And he went."*
- Mid: a hollow in the slag, drag marks into the dark. *"This is where he fell. Somebody moved him."*
- Closing: the Wraith leaving under lit chimneys; Rook holding a log page with the brand. *Cliffhanger: an unknown mark, and someone took Howl.*

## 7. Three signature pictures
1. Foundry 4 by furnace light: a tiny Rook with a torch beside the Wraith, staring at an empty hollow in the slag.
2. Storm: the Thresher's four red knees over the dig camp, Rook's truck on its side, an old man in a long coat walking toward it.
3. Sunset, the quiet field: the Wraith, Howler-07's wolf plate on its shoulder, pulling a plough as two old people watch from a tailgate.
