# Pitch C: The World and the Pictures
## "A Hand on Every Machine"

### 1. Logline
2176. FORGE's sealed war zones are open, and the county digs glowing cores out of the wheat until the wrecks start walking. A farm kid in a dug-up mech learns that the man waking FORGE once piloted that same mech, and that the red lamps lighting every town are his reins.

### 2. The world in 2176
1. **The Seal and the Hand Law.** The 2127 Accord fenced FORGE's zones for fifty years and made one law: *no machine moves without a hand on it.*
2. **Embers.** Every FORGE machine holds a melon-sized core of red glass, still warm. Crews *quench* it in blue brine barrels (it hisses into steam), which turns it grey: just a battery that heats a farmhouse for a winter. Selling a live red core is illegal. Kids chant *"Red eye, run. Grey eye, done."*
3. **Who buys.** Towns buy grey cores. **Vane Light & Quench** pays triple for red ones: "live glass makes a warmer lamp."
4. **Dig crews.** Each crew flies its claims-office number on a flag atop a whip-pole. They camp under tarps between a dead Gunner's ribs.
5. **Wreck farms.** A Thresher's leg is a silo and wheat grows through Tank tracks. On frosty mornings old wrecks "tick."
6. **Vane lamps.** In the hungry Lean Years after FORGE's farm machines stopped, the Lamplighter put a red lamp mast in every town. Old folks whisper, "FORGE fed us."
7. **Harbor 9.** A boom town, where a chalk board reads *HOUND 40 · GUNNER 300 · LIVE: ASK AT VANE*. The rusting Howler-07 in the square is Howl's memorial. Howl was buried last winter.
8. **The Bone Stands.** The burned pines have grown back taller than a Gunner, up through mossy wrecks.
9. **Foundry 4.** Guarded until this spring. FORGE's eye is still in its tower, cracked in a star, with swallows nesting in the crack.

### 3. The truth
**2126.** Rook had two pilots: Howl (Unit 07) and **Silas Varga, callsign WRAITH, Unit 09**. A week before Foundry 4, a Thresher swallowed Unit 09, and FORGE carried Silas off to study "the hand." Rook searched one night, then sent Howl in instead. When the eye broke, Silas walked out in the smoke with pockets full of live cores, knowing how a core *listens*. He was listed dead.

**2127–2175.** As **Silas Vane, "the Lamplighter,"** he lit the Lean Years and became a hero. Every lamp mast is a relay. In his Harbor 9 quench house, secretly a print shop, he built a **bridle** to steer the pieces of FORGE. FORGE, he believes, was only wrong to have no hand on it. *"The law says a hand on every machine. Mine is."*

**Why now.** The Seal ends and the garrison leaves. The rush brings live cores to his door by the thousand. Howl is dead and Rook is old: he waited for his enemies to age. His machines scare crews off their fields, and Vane buys the empty claims the next morning. The rush is really a harvest, and as FORGE comes back together, it starts steering him. Every UNKNOWN line in chapter 1 already fits: he knows Rook by name, and he calls the pilot by his own old callsign.

### 4. Characters
- **Silas Vane, 72 (villain).** Tall and stooped, in a long bone-white coat, with silver hair tied back and a red lantern on his belt. One white glove. **His mark is a white open hand with FORGE's gear-and-eye in the palm.** *Talks* softly, like a county-fair preacher, using first names and never shouting. Later, FORGE's CAPITALS creep in.
- **Rook, 74. The same Rook.** Small and wiry, with cropped white hair, a taped 2126 headset, a faded wolf patch and glasses on a cord. She drives a battered green crane truck. *Talks* in clipped, dry radio discipline, with rare warmth ("Good. Again."). She argued for the Seal "until we're wiser." *"We weren't wiser. Just older."*
- **Ezra "Ez" Pike, 19 (pilot).** A farm kid. At 14 he found Unit 09 in the grain bin of the fallen Thresher his family's house leans on, with the seat empty, and spent five years getting it to walk. Rook hired the county's only mech. *Look:* lanky and sunburnt, with curls under a cut-down helmet painted Wraith orange, patched overalls and a wrench in his pocket. *Talks* little ("Copy." "Yes ma'am."), which leaves room for the player.
- **Hattie Brask, foreman of Crew 4.** Big and loud, with cutting goggles pushed up. Her daughter **Juno (11)** runs messages between camps by bicycle. *Talks* in jokes under fire: "Twitching isn't walking!"

### 5. The arc
**Ch 1, The Salvage Rush (as built).** It ends at a relit Foundry 4 and a log signed with the hand mark.

**Ch 2, Red Glass (Harbor 9 at sunset and in rain; fields).** Ez shows Rook a faded white handprint inside the Wraith's hatch: the mark without the eye. Rook says only "Silas." Trail a live-core convoy into Vane's quench house and topple lamp masts as Hornets launch from them. Vane bought every emptied field. The chapter ends on a line of bone-white machines: *"Hello, Rook. You left me in the field."*

**Ch 3, Fifty Winters (woods in fog and at night; fields in a storm).** Wrecks walk home to Foundry 4, a road of red eyes across the wheat. Lancers and the Stag hold the Bone Stands. Rook confesses. **The Pike Thresher stands up with the farmhouse on its back**, and Ez must bring down his own home. Silas slips: *"THE FIELDS BELONG TO... to me, Rook."*

**Ch 4, A Hand on Every Machine (a storm-hit coast town, then Foundry 4).** Silas wires himself into a relit eye. Every numbered crew brings its brine. Escort Rook's tanker past the Maw and Tanks, then hold the eye while the crews pump. **Final picture: the Wraith's orange hand lifting old Silas out of the steaming grey eye**, with Rook below. Epilogue: the Pikes replant. *"The fields belong to the people who plant them."*

### 6. Chapter 1 scene outline
**M1 Dig Site**
- *Open:* Dawn wheat, the Pike farmhouse built against a fallen Thresher's claw, the Wraith kneeling by the barn. Sets up the world and home.
- *Mid:* Hattie holds a red core over a steaming brine barrel while a wreck's eye flickers behind her. Explains embers.
- *Close:* Toppled masts at dusk, their Vane lamps still glowing in the mud. Plants the lamps in plain sight.

**M2 Harbor Nine**
- *Open:* The midnight core market under red lamps and its chalk price board. Shows who buys.
- *Mid:* A burst spawner pod in a crate with a sea-freight tag. Delivered, not dug up.
- *Close:* The Wraith hangs from the crane hook above Howl's statue. "Last mech I lifted out of here was Howl's."

**M3 Pine Wake**
- *Open:* The Bone Stands, with Crew 7's empty camp and its beacon blinking. The woods fifty years on.
- *Mid:* Crew 7 in blankets in Rook's truck while rust-red eyes watch from the trees. The voice knows Rook.
- *Close:* The dead Alpha Hound at sunset, its fresh welds stamped 2176. "Born this year."

**M4 Creek Road**
- *Open:* Fog. Brine barrels on Rook's truck, the Wraith's amber lights alongside. "Nine walked this road in 2126."
- *Mid:* Headlights catch the Brood Skitter rising from the creek. The danger grows.
- *Close:* A white-coated Vane buyer offers triple "if they're still red." Rook tips the cores into the creek in a burst of steam.

**M5 Storm Relays**
- *Open:* Lightning over the fields as crews flee with torn flags. Fear is winning.
- *Mid:* Through Rook's windshield, Gunner Prime is lit bone-white by lightning. "Meet my newest."
- *Close:* Gunner Prime fallen in wet wheat, Ez in the open hatch. "I helped make that foundry cold."

**M6 Foundry Four**
- *Open:* The foundry from the ridge: a dark cracked eye and chimneys pouring red smoke. "I was here the night Howl broke it."
- *Mid:* Rook works by flashlight at the log terminal, with half-built Hounds hanging on chains behind her.
- *Close:* The log's white-hand mark in the foreground. Behind the Wraith, the eye gives one red flicker.

### 7. Visual style
- **Medium:** painted cinematic stills (gouache and oil, visible brushwork), like a 1970s sci-fi paperback cover meeting game key art. No text in the picture.
- **Palette:** wheat gold, slate, rust and charcoal. Only Wraith orange and amber, FORGE red and Vane bone white are saturated. Brine steam is cold blue.
- **Light:** one strong source per frame: low sun, fog glow, core glow or lightning.
- **Framing:** 1536×1024, wide and low, with people small against huge machines. Keep the key detail in the middle band and the bottom third dark for the text.
