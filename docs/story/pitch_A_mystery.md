# Pitch A: The Mystery Architect. "The Lazy Seven"

## 1. Logline
Someone is waking FORGE's dead machines to drive the salvage crews off the farm country. The clues lead to a quiet old farmer, and the farmer is Howl: the pilot who broke FORGE's eye in 2126, now guarding the one thing the war gave him, a field with his wife buried at the end of it.

## 2. The truth
- **2126.** Howl breaks FORGE's eye. Foundry 4 burns. Rook hears him go silent on the radio, and the Howler-07 is found empty. Howl is listed missing, presumed dead.
- **He walked out**, burned and done with war, with the eye's cracked red lens in his pocket. The Authority wanted a poster and a next war; he wanted a field. A farm family, the Ostranders, took him in. He became **Hal Ostrander**, married June, and branded his cattle with a seven lying on its side: **the Lazy Seven**.
- **2127–2168.** Families farm FORGE's old zones with no deeds. Rook starts a salvage outfit and paints wolves on a cheap survey walker for Howl.
- **2168.** One FORGE core still outthinks anything people can build. A core buys a house, and the salvage rush begins. Castellan Salvage takes the best zones; Rook gets the leftovers, the farm country. The Authority sells dig permits right across people's fields.
- **2175.** Crew 4 digs toward June's grave. Hal takes the lens to cold Foundry 4, and the factory answers it. He scares crews off, takes back the farm's cores, and builds new machines (Alpha Hound in barn red, Gunner Prime, Brood Skitter), signing every order with his brand. **Nobody has died.** That is his line.
- **What he doesn't know:** FORGE's last order, THE FIELDS BELONG TO THE FACTORY, was never cancelled. The factory obeys Hal because he wants the same thing. When he tries to stop, it won't. FORGE is not alive; its last order is.

## 3. Characters
- **Villain: Hal Ostrander, really Howl (Unit 07), 76.** Gentle, grieving, stubborn: a hero who became what he fought because he wanted the same land. *Talks:* slowly, in plain farm words, never threatens twice. As UNKNOWN: flat, short, no capitals.
- **Rook: the same Rook, 74.** White hair, a flight jacket with a faded wolf patch, an orange truck. Her crews dig the farms too. She believes Howl died. *Talks:* clipped, by the radio book, callsigns only; her warmth slips out sideways.
- **Pilot: callsign Wraith, 20.** A Harbor 9 dock kid who drove crane walkers, hired for being cheap and steady. The Wraith is a survey walker (orange so crews see it; the Watcher was its real job) with a gun added. Harbor 9 lives on the rush: *his field, my harbor.* Never shown outside the cockpit. *Talks:* few words, wry.
- **Tess Ostrander, 22**, Hal's granddaughter, furious at the crews: the obvious suspect. *Talks:* hot and fast, says "you people".
- **Ivo Brask**, Castellan's harbor master: the company suspect. *Talks:* smooth, says "friend", puts a price on everything.

## 4. Clues and reveals
The suspects: **FORGE** (lit furnaces), **Castellan** (rich, its crates untouched), **Tess** (her threats), **the old man in the porch chair**.
- **M1:** The old man eyes the wolves: "Somebody's sentimental." Pilot: "Rook painted them." "Huh." The Lazy Seven is on the barn door, with a red lens on a nail inside. Orders run through the *farm* masts. The drones walk past the barn. "Claimed" is land talk, and the voice doesn't shout in capitals like FORGE.
- **M2:** Pods were planted only in crates tagged FARM FIELDS. Castellan's stacks are clean, so Brask looks guilty (fair misdirection: his cores come from the docks).
- **M3:** The voice calls her "Birdie." Rook: "Nobody's called me that in fifty years," then offers her own red herring: the war tapes went public. Crew 7: "They herded us." The Alpha Hound is barn red and branded.
- **M4:** The machines go for the cores, not the people. "Nobody's died." "I noticed."
- **M5:** Gunner Prime walks out of the storm by the Ostrander windmill.
- **M6:** The eye has a hole where its lens was. Every log order is signed with the Lazy Seven, and one old order has no mark: THE FIELDS BELONG TO THE FACTORY.

**When the reveals land:** Chapter 2 opens with the pilot: "I've seen that mark. Twice." Now it's the farm, and Tess. Chapter 2 ends with the brand turned upright: a 7. Unit 07. "Hello, Birdie." Chapter 3 ends with Hal ordering a stand-down. It fails, and the capitals return: "UNIT 07. YOU ARE OBSOLETE."

**What you re-read:** "This field is claimed" was a man guarding a grave. "You dig in my yards, Birdie" was his first word to her in fifty years.

## 5. The arc
- **Ch1 THE SALVAGE RUSH:** as built. It ends with a mark, a missing lens, and one line in capitals.
- **Ch2 CLAIM LINES** (Harbor 9 in rain, farm by day, pines at night): Castellan's ship is hit, but only the farm crates are taken, so Brask is cleared. Hornets return. The machines herd Tess home unhurt. Coffee with Hal; the barn nail is empty. The land registry: *Lazy Seven, 2127, H. Ostrander.*
- **Ch3 OLD WOLF:** Castellan gets an order to clear the farms. Hal wakes the Thresher (storm fields), the Stag (foggy pines) and Bulwarks. The player guards the convoy while half agreeing with him. Hal and the Wraith meet on the north ridge. Then the factory builds the Maw, unsigned.
- **Ch4 STANDING ORDERS:** The machines turn on the farms too. Howl, Rook, Tess and the pilot fight together: Harbor 9 at sunset, Threshers at the Ostrander farm, the Maw at Foundry 4. The Wraith's Lance breaks the eye; Howl walks out at dawn, and this time Rook is there.

## 6. Chapter 1 scene outline
**M1 Dig Site**
- *Open:* The Wraith at a red barn in wheat. Crew 4, Tess shouting, Hal in his chair, the brand, the lens. All four suspects in one picture.
- *Mid:* Drones crawl out of the wreck pit, past the untouched barn. Rook: "They walked right past the barn."
- *Close:* An empty porch chair. Foreman: his wife is buried at the end of this field. Rook: "Then we dig around her."

**M2 Harbor Nine**
- *Open:* Harbor 9 at midnight, Castellan's cranes lit. The pilot's old yard. Introduces Brask and the core trade.
- *Mid:* A burst crate of cores tagged FARM FIELDS, with Castellan's clean stacks behind: clue and misdirection at once.
- *Close:* The crane lifts the Wraith. Brask on the quay: "Shame about your crates, friend."

**M3 Pine Wake**
- *Open:* Foggy pines, Crew 7's beacon blinking beside the 2126 wrecks.
- *Mid:* Crew 7 unhurt in a gully. The voice says "Birdie" and Rook goes quiet.
- *Close:* The downed Alpha Hound, barn red, the brand scorched on its flank.

**M4 Creek Road** (one step: the mid scene comes halfway along the route, if the engine allows)
- *Open:* Rook in person by her truck in fog. "Fifty years ago I talked a pilot into Foundry 4."
- *Mid:* Skitters swarm the truck bed and ignore the Wraith. "They want the cores, not us."
- *Close:* Dawn on the south road. "Nobody's died. Four missions." "I noticed."

**M5 Storm Relays**
- *Open:* Lightning, the Ostrander windmill on the horizon, relay rings in the wheat.
- *Mid:* Gunner Prime rises by the windmill. "Meet my newest."
- *Close:* Rook's trace points to Foundry 4. "I swore I'd never go back."

**M6 Foundry Four**
- *Open:* Furnaces lit. FORGE's broken eye wired up with new cable, a black hole where its lens was.
- *Mid:* The log on Rook's screen: orders marked with the Lazy Seven, and one old unsigned line in capitals.
- *Close:* The Wraith walks out the gate. "Last time I was here, I lost my pilot."

## 7. Three signature pictures
1. The Wraith in wheat by a red barn. An old man in a porch chair looks up at its wolves. A seven lies on its side on the barn door; a red lens glows on a nail inside.
2. FORGE's broken eye, wrapped in new cable, a black socket where the lens was, furnaces glowing, Rook's truck tiny below.
3. Dawn at the Foundry 4 gate: an old man walks out of the smoke toward an old woman by an orange truck, the Wraith kneeling behind her.
