# Ironhowl 4 · Chapter 1 · The Salvage Rush — the script

## Mission 1 · Dig Site

**Mission card:** The Calloway farm. Crew 4 is digging FORGE’s old wrecks out of their own wheat. Meet Rook at the barn, then guard the dig. The Wraith is the only mech Crew 4 has.

### Opening scene
*Picture 1 of 2: `m1_open`*

> *2176. For fifty years FORGE’s war zones were fenced off. Now the fence is down, and the rush is on.*
>
> *Every dead machine out there holds a core of red glass, still warm. One core heats a farmhouse all winter.*
>
> **BESS:** Live ones bite. Dunk them in brine till they hiss and go grey. Then they’re safe.
>
> **PIP:** Red eye, run! Grey eye, done!

*Picture 2 of 2: `m1_open2`*

> **PIP:** Wolves are done, Jo! Same wolves Howl had when he broke FORGE. Now it looks like a real war mech.
>
> **JO:** It’s a survey mech, Pip. Right, Gran?
>
> **BESS:** Not today. The co-op’s sending a handler out. Calls herself Rook.
>
> **PIP:** Bet she’s never seen wolves like these.

**Radio during “Meet Rook at the barn”:** Rook: Wraith, this is Rook. I’m parked by the barn. Walk on over. · Rook: Left thumb walks, right thumb aims. Tap JET to hop, hold it to hover.

**Radio during “Guard the dig”:** Dig Crew: Rook! Something just twitched in the wreck pile. · Rook: Scrap doesn’t twitch. Wraith, get in the ring and hold.

### Middle scene (before “Knock out the relay masts”)
*Picture: `m1_mid`*

> **ROOK:** Look at the wrecks. Every eye’s turned to the masts on the ridge. Somebody’s talking to them.
>
> **UNKNOWN:** Crew 4. This field is claimed. Leave it.
>
> **BESS:** Claimed? My dad was born in that field.
>
> **ROOK:** Wraith. Knock those masts flat.

**Radio during “Knock out the relay masts”:** Rook: Three masts. The marker shows the nearest. Keep firing till it falls. · UNKNOWN: Take your people home, Crew 4.

### Closing scene
*Picture: `m1_close`*

> **ROOK:** Masts are down. Not bad shooting, Wraith.
>
> **JO:** I’m a spotter, Rook. I find things. I don’t usually blow them up.
>
> **ROOK:** Then find me whoever was talking through them. Bring it home, Wraith.
>
> **JO:** Bring what home? I’m standing in my own yard.
>
> **ROOK:** …Never mind. Old habit.

## Mission 2 · Harbor Nine

**Mission card:** Harbor 9 at midnight: the boom town where every dig crew sells its cores. Somebody planted spawner pods in the container stacks overnight. Find all four.

### Opening scene
*Picture: `m2_open`*

> **BESS:** Our crates sail on the morning boat. That’s a new barn roof, right there.
>
> **PIP:** A Gunner core’s worth three hundred! What’s “LIVE: ASK AT VANE” mean?
>
> **BESS:** A red core nobody quenched. Selling those is against the law. Silas Vane pays triple anyway.
>
> **ROOK:** Man in the white coat. If he calls you friend, count your fingers.

**Radio during “Get into the yard”:** Rook: Stick to the lanes. Those stacks are too tall to hop, even for you.

**Radio during “Destroy the spawner pods”:** Rook: Pods print Skitters till you break them. The marker shows the nearest. · UNKNOWN: Those crates came off claimed ground.

### Middle scene (before “Get to the middle crane”)
*Picture: `m2_mid`*

> **JO:** Rook. All four pods were hidden in farm crates. Ours. Crew 2’s. Crew 6’s.
>
> **ROOK:** And Vane’s?
>
> **JO:** Right across the lane. Not one pod. Not a scratch.
>
> **BESS:** Scare the farm crews off, then buy our fields cheap. That’s Vane all over.
>
> **ROOK:** Maybe. Vane’s a crook, but he’s a careful one.

**Radio during “Get to the middle crane”:** Rook: Gunner on the quay! Get under the middle crane. I’m lifting you out.

### Closing scene
*Picture: `m2_close`*

> **VANE:** Lovely lift, Rook. Shame about all those farm crates, friend.
>
> **VANE:** When Crew 4 gets tired of digging, I’ll buy that field. Fair price.
>
> **BESS:** Fair like the place across the creek? Your diggers flattened that house in a day.
>
> **VANE:** Old houses come down, Mrs. Calloway. Goodnight, friends.
>
> **ROOK:** Silas. You’re standing under my hook.

## Mission 3 · Pine Wake

**Mission card:** Crew 7 has gone quiet in the pine woods, out by a field of 2126 wrecks. Find them, guard Rook while she loads them up, and watch the trees for the pack.

### Opening scene
*Picture: `m3_open`*

> *The pine woods burned in the war. Now they stand taller than a Gunner, and the wrecks wear moss.*
>
> **ROOK:** No word from Crew 7 since sunup. A dad and two kids, digging by the old wreck field.
>
> **JO:** Shouldn’t you wait back at the road?
>
> **ROOK:** In 2126 I sat in a bunker and listened. I’m done sitting.

**Radio during “Find Crew 7”:** Rook: Their beacon’s east of the trail. Watch the trees. · Rook: Hounds crouch, then charge dead straight. Sidestep or hop.

**Radio during “Guard Rook”:** Rook: Found them. All three alive, and grumpy. Give me one minute to load up. · Rook: Keep them off my truck, Wraith. Stay in the ring.

### Middle scene (before “Bring down the Alpha Hound”)
*Picture: `m3_mid`*

> **UNKNOWN:** Get your crew out of my yards, Birdie.
>
> *Rook doesn’t answer. For a long moment, she doesn’t move.*
>
> **JO:** Rook? Who’s Birdie?
>
> **ROOK:** Nobody’s called me that in fifty years.

**Radio during “Bring down the Alpha Hound”:** Rook: Eyes up. Rust-red one coming out of the trees, twice the size. · Rook: Clear its pack first, then keep moving. It charges harder.

**Radio during “Reach the north ridge”:** Rook: Crew 7’s clear. Meet me up on the north ridge.

### Closing scene
*Picture: `m3_close`*

> **JO:** Rook, about Birdie…
>
> **ROOK:** Look at these welds instead. Bright. Clean. This thing was built this year.
>
> **JO:** FORGE is dead. So who’s building new ones?
>
> **PIP:** Did you get the big one? I’m calling it Biscuit!
>
> **ROOK:** We are not calling it Biscuit.
>
> **PIP:** Too late.

## Mission 4 · Creek Road

**Mission card:** Night fog on the creek road. Rook is driving Crew 7 and their cores out of the woods. Escort her truck to the south road. If the truck is destroyed, the mission fails.

### Opening scene
*Picture: `m4_open`*

> **CREW 7:** Miss Rook? What’s the pilot’s real name?
>
> **ROOK:** Wraith.
>
> **JO:** It’s Jo.
>
> **ROOK:** Copy, Wraith.

**Radio during “Escort Rook”:** Rook: I only roll while you’re close, Wraith. Stay within 35 metres. · Rook: Some of them go for my truck. Kill those first. · UNKNOWN: Leave the cores on the road and walk home.

### Middle scene (halfway down the road)
*Picture: `m4_mid`*

> **CREW 7:** Who’s the man in the photo? His mech’s got wolves on it. Like yours.
>
> **ROOK:** Howl. My pilot. He never came out of Foundry 4.
>
> **JO:** My kid brother copied our wolves off an old poster of him.
>
> **ROOK:** Pip got the ears wrong.

### Closing scene
*Picture: `m4_close`*

> **ROOK:** You stayed close. Most don’t.
>
> **JO:** That’s four fights now. Nobody’s been hurt. Not once.
>
> **ROOK:** I noticed. They chase folks off, they take the cores, and they let everyone walk.
>
> **ROOK:** FORGE never let anyone walk.

## Mission 5 · Storm Relays

**Mission card:** A storm over the farm fields. The voice reaches its machines through three relays. Take all three relays: hold each ring until it’s full. Then Rook can trace the signal.

### Opening scene
*Picture: `m5_open`*

> *A storm rolls over farm country. Three more crews have packed up and left this week.*
>
> **BESS:** Crew 2 sold their claim to Vane this morning. For next to nothing.
>
> **ROOK:** Then we find the voice before anyone else sells. It talks through relays. Relays can be traced.
>
> **JO:** So I stand in the lightning while you sit in the truck.
>
> **ROOK:** I’m seventy-four, Wraith. Everything on me is out of warranty. I get the truck.

**Radio during “Take the relays”:** Rook: Stand in each ring till it fills. Never mind the lightning. It only hits them. · UNKNOWN: Copy, Wraith. Trace all you like. Then go home.

### Middle scene (before “Bring down Gunner Prime”)
*Picture: `m5_mid`*

> **ROOK:** Got it. The signal runs straight back to one place. Foundry 4.
>
> **JO:** Where Howl broke FORGE? That place has been cold since the war.
>
> **ROOK:** Not any more.

**Radio during “Bring down Gunner Prime”:** Rook: Something huge on the east road. Bone white. Use cover between bursts. · UNKNOWN: Meet my newest.

### Closing scene
*Picture: `m5_close`*

> **PIP:** That’s three big ones down! I’m calling this one Marshmallow.
>
> **ROOK:** We are still not doing names.
>
> **ROOK:** Get some sleep, all of you. Tomorrow we drive to Foundry 4.
>
> **BESS:** Rook. You don’t have to be the one who goes.
>
> **ROOK:** Fifty years I’ve stayed away from that gate, Bess. That’s long enough.

## Mission 6 · Foundry Four

**Mission card:** The signal leads to Foundry 4: FORGE’s old heart, fenced off and cold since 2126. Its furnaces are lit again. Hold the yard while Rook pulls the factory log.

### Opening scene
*Picture: `m6_open`*

> *A plaque by the gate: UNITS 01–07 · 2126 · THEY HELD THE LINE.*
>
> **ROOK:** Seven pilots. I learned every one of their names.
>
> **JO:** Is that why I’m Wraith?
>
> **ROOK:** Eyes front, Wraith. Let’s go knock.

**Radio during “Enter the foundry yard”:** Rook: Furnaces are hot. Somebody’s been building in here.

**Radio during “Hold the yard”:** Rook: Ninety seconds to pull the log. Hold the yard, Wraith. · UNKNOWN: Turn around. There’s nothing left in here for you.

### Middle scene (before “Break out”)
*Picture: `m6_mid`*

> **ROOK:** Got the log. Wraith… look down. By your left foot.
>
> *A hollow in the slag, the shape of a fallen mech. It’s empty. Drag marks lead off into the dark.*
>
> **ROOK:** Howl’s mech went down right here. I said go, and he went.
>
> **ROOK:** We left him lying here. Somebody moved him.

**Radio during “Break out”:** Rook: Two big ones at the gate. Fine, Pip. Biscuit and Marshmallow.

**Radio during “Get back to the gate”:** Rook: Gate’s clear! Bring it home, Wraith. I’m right behind you. · Rook: Don’t you dare stop for me.

### Closing scene (the end of chapter 1)
*Picture 1 of 2: `m6_close`*

> **ROOK:** The log names no one. Just orders. Hounds, Gunners, things I’ve never seen.
>
> **ROOK:** Every order’s signed with the same mark. A seven, lying on its side.
>
> **JO:** A cattle brand?
>
> **ROOK:** Not one I know.

*Picture 2 of 2: `m6_close2`*

> **JO:** Look at the eye. The crack’s been stitched with new cable.
>
> **ROOK:** I heard that eye break.
>
> *It flickers red. One more line crawls across Rook’s screen, older than the rest. No mark.*
>
> *THE FIELDS BELONG TO THE FACTORY.*
