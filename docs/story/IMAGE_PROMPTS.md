You'll make 26 pictures: 6 character sheets first, then the 20 story pictures in the order they play.
Each grey box is one prompt. Copy all of it and paste it into ChatGPT.

---

## How to do it

1. **Unzip the reference pictures** Claude gave you. You'll use these 8 files:

   | File | What it shows |
   |---|---|
   | `hd_front.png`, `hd_side.png`, `hd_rear.png` | the Wraith (front, side, back) |
   | `D01_gunner_front.png` | a FORGE Gunner (Gunner Prime is built from it too) |
   | `E01_hound_front.png`, `E02_hound_side.png` | a FORGE Hound (the Alpha Hound is built from it too) |
   | `F01_skitter_front.png` | a Skitter |
   | `F05_spawnerpod_front.png` | a spawner pod |

2. **Make the 6 character sheets first** (Rook, Bess, Pip, Jo, Silas Vane, Rook's truck). Use a **new chat** for each one,
   paste the prompt, and attach nothing. If one looks wrong, ask again until you like it. Save them as
   `char_rook.png`, `char_bess.png`, `char_pip.png`, `char_jo.png`, `char_vane.png`, `char_truck.png`.
   They keep everyone looking the same in all 20 pictures.
3. **Then make the 20 pictures in order.** For each one, open a **new chat**, attach every file listed after
   **Attach:**, paste the prompt and send. If ChatGPT says you attached too many, leave out the ones marked *(optional)*.
4. **The shape must be wide: 3:2 landscape, 1536 × 1024.** If it comes out square or tall, reply:
   `Make it 3:2 landscape, 1536 × 1024. Keep everything else the same.`
5. **Check it before you save it.** You're looking for: the white "09" on the Wraith's chest is the **only** writing
   in the picture (it's really on the mech, so keep it); the small white wolf's head is on its shoulder plate (not a
   second "09"); no other words, letters or numbers anywhere; Jo's face never shows; the important stuff is in the
   middle; the bottom third is plain and darkish. If ChatGPT added any other lettering, reply:
   `Remove all text, letters and numbers from the picture, except the white "09" on the mech's chest. Change nothing else.`
6. **Save it under its exact name** (for example `m1_open.png`) and send it to Claude. You can send them a few at a time
   or all at once.
7. **About the middle band:** your phone held sideways is wider than the picture, so about **1/6 of the top and 1/6 of
   the bottom get cut off**, and the words sit over the lower part. That's why every prompt keeps the important stuff
   in the middle. If something important ends up near the top or bottom edge, reply:
   `Zoom out a little and move everything important toward the middle. Keep the top and bottom edges plain.`

**More quick fixes** (paste the line into the same chat):
- People too big: `Make the people much smaller next to the machines. The mech is 6.6 metres tall; a person's head only reaches its knee.`
- Jo's face shows: `Jo always wears the orange helmet with a dark visor. The face must never show.`
- Looks like a photo or 3D render: `Make it look painted: gouache and oil, visible brushstrokes, like a 1970s paperback cover.`
- The Wraith looks different from the reference: `Match the attached orange mech exactly: same head, armour, colours and bird-like legs.`
