# Ironhowl 4: concept boards (quick looks, not for modelling)

## For Christopher
These boards are for deciding what could go into the game. They're quick looks, so nothing gets built from them. If you
like something, it goes into a proper HD request afterwards, like the kit and foes request. In one ChatGPT chat, attach the
three Wraith V2 HD pictures for style, paste everything below the line, and ask for the five boards in a zip named
`ironhowl4_concept_boards.zip`.

---

## For ChatGPT
Make **five concept boards** for **Ironhowl 4**, a mech shooter set in 2176. Each board is one landscape image
(1536 × 1024) holding up to 10 items. Draw each item as a clean three-quarter view on a plain light-grey background, with a
short name label and a size in metres. Keep them quick and readable rather than detailed.

The player's mech is the Wraith (attached): worn orange armour, a charcoal frame, white stripes and amber lights. The enemy
is FORGE: unmanned machines in dark charcoal and gunmetal, worn through to red primer, with glowing red eyes and lights and a gear-with-an-eye
emblem. They have no cockpits and no orange.

**Board 1: more FORGE machines** (FORGE style, 10 items, two designs each):
1. **Hornet:** a flying machine that dives from above.
2. **Lancer:** a sniper that paints you with a red laser line, then fires.
3. **Bulwark:** carries a big shield and blocks shots for the others.
4. **Tank:** tracked, with a heavy turret.
5. **Mortar:** a crawler that lobs shells from far away.

**Board 2: bosses** (FORGE style):
1. **Thresher:** a 16 m harvester walker with four glowing red knees, its weak points.
2. **Stag:** a walker with two antler weapon pods and a furnace in its chest.
3. **Maw:** a huge jaw that has to open before its core can be hit.
4. **FORGE itself:** a foundry machine with one great eye, guarded by shield pylons.
5. **Gunner Prime:** a heavy two-legged gun walker with a big cannon on each arm, bone-white paint and red lights.
6. **Alpha Hound:** a four-legged hunter with oversized horns and spine blades, rust red.
7. **Brood Skitter:** a large four-legged drone carrying a spawner pod on its back, black with glowing red seams.

**Board 3: mission objects and pickups**, 10 items:
1. FORGE relay mast
2. gate control box
3. mooring winch
4. searchlight tower
5. shield pylon
6. capture relay ring
7. Rook's salvage truck, a 2176 off-road truck
8. supply drop crate
9. pickups: gun chip, repair, shield, missiles, fuel
10. scrap pile from a wreck

**Board 4: places for 2176**, 8 small landscape scenes, each with the Wraith in it for scale:
1. overgrown farm fields
2. a flooded harbour container yard at night
3. burnt pine woods
4. a FORGE foundry
5. a storm-hit coastal town
6. a dead city of half-built towers
7. a desert solar farm
8. a mountain pass in snow

**Board 5: the upgrade bay and intel brief**, 4 screen mock-ups for a phone held upright:
1. the upgrade bay, with the Wraith turning in the middle and one kit card open;
2. an armory-book page for one weapon;
3. the intel brief ("10 Skitters headed your way") with a foe turning in a HUD panel;
4. the mission HUD.

Keep every screen uncluttered: big readable numbers and only a few controls.

Name the files `board1_forge_machines.png`, `board2_bosses.png`, `board3_mission_objects.png`, `board4_places.png` and
`board5_screens.png`.
